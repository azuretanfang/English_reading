📖 12 Invention of printing

印刷术的发明 - 英语精读笔记

📖原文精读

A Chinese named Bi Sheng invented movable type print around 1,000 years ago.
一位名叫毕昇的中国人在大约1000年前发明了活字印刷术。
The movable components are cubes made of baked clay.
可移动的组件是由烧制粘土制成的立方体。
Characters were carved on each piece of clay, which looked like the seals widely used in China.
每个粘土块上都刻有字符，看起来像中国广泛使用的印章。
The movable components could be arranged freely to form different vocabularies and sentences.
可移动组件可以自由排列，组成不同的词汇和句子。
They were fixed on an iron plate with frames in certain sequence.
它们按照特定顺序被固定在带框架的铁板上。
Then ink was painted and a piece of paper was laid down.
然后涂上墨水，铺上一张纸。
A printed sheet was created this way.
通过这种方式创建了一张印刷页。
This technique improved the efficiency of printing and transformed traditional transcription into modern printing.
这项技术提高了印刷效率，将传统抄写转变为现代印刷。
Scripts and paintings spread widely in the world with the help of yìn shuā.
文字和绘画在印刷术的帮助下在世界范围内广泛传播。

📖逐句精读（共9句）

1A Chinese named Bi Sheng invented movable type print around 1,000 years ago.
一位名叫毕昇的中国人在大约1000年前发明了活字印刷术。
🔤 句型：主语（含后置定语）+ 谓语 + 宾语 + 时间状语
A Chinese named Bi Sheng：named Bi Sheng 是过去分词短语作后置定语，修饰 A Chinese，相当于 "who was named Bi Sheng"。

📌 过去分词作后置定语：named = "被命名为"，这种结构替代定语从句，更简洁。
类似：a book written in English（一本用英文写的书）
movable type print 活字印刷 around 大约
📌 词汇：
• movable /ˈmuːvəbl/ adj. 可移动的
• invent /ɪnˈvent/ v. 发明（inventor 发明者；invention 发明物）
2The movable components are cubes made of baked clay.
可移动的组件是由烧制粘土制成的立方体。
🔤 句型：主系表结构 + 过去分词后置定语
The movable components 主语 → are 系动词 → cubes made of baked clay 表语

📌 made of vs. made from：
• made of：能看出原材料（成品保留原材料特征）→ cubes made of clay（能看出是粘土）
• made from：原材料发生了化学变化 → paper made from wood pulp
📌 词汇：
• component /kəmˈpoʊnənt/ n. 组件，部件
• cube /kjuːb/ n. 立方体
• baked clay /beɪkt kleɪ/ n. 烧制的粘土（陶土）
3Characters were carved on each piece of clay, which looked like the seals widely used in China.
每个粘土块上都刻有字符，看起来像中国广泛使用的印章。
🔤 句型：被动语态 + 非限制性定语从句
主句：Characters were carved on each piece of clay（被动语态：be + 过去分词）
非限制性定语从句：which looked like the seals widely used in China
• which 指代前面整个主句的内容（即"刻有字的粘土块"）
• widely used in China 是过去分词短语，后置修饰 seals

📌 注意：非限制性定语从句前有逗号，补充说明，不是限定范围。
📌 词汇：
• carve /kɑːrv/ v. 雕刻，刻（carve → carved → carved）
• seal /siːl/ n. 印章；v. 封闭（此处用名词义）
4The movable components could be arranged freely to form different vocabularies and sentences.
可移动的组件可以自由排列，组成不同的词汇和句子。
🔤 句型：情态动词 + 被动语态 + 不定式目的状语
could be arranged：情态动词 could + be + 过去分词 = 情态被动
to form different vocabularies and sentences：不定式作目的状语，表示"为了……"

📌 freely：副词，修饰 arranged，表示"自由地"。这正是活字印刷最核心的创新点——字模可以自由组合。
📌 词汇：
• arrange /əˈreɪndʒ/ v. 排列，安排（arrangement n.）
• vocabulary /voʊˈkæbjəleri/ n. 词汇（复数：vocabularies）
5They were fixed on an iron plate with frames in certain sequence.
它们按照特定顺序被固定在带框架的铁板上。
🔤 句型：被动语态 + 介词短语
They were fixed on an iron plate：被动语态，they 指代上句的 movable components
with frames：介词短语，修饰 iron plate，说明铁板带有框架
in certain sequence：介词短语作状语，表示排列顺序

📌 介词辨析：
• in sequence 按顺序 / in certain sequence 按特定顺序
• fix on 固定在……上
📌 词汇：
• iron plate /ˈaɪərn pleɪt/ n. 铁板
• frame /freɪm/ n. 框架
• sequence /ˈsiːkwəns/ n. 顺序，序列
6Then ink was painted and a piece of paper was laid down.
然后涂上墨水，铺上一张纸。
🔤 句型：并列被动句 + 时间副词
Then：时间副词，表示紧接着上一步之后
两个被动语态并列：ink was painted 和 a piece of paper was laid down

📌 动词辨析：
• paint 此处作"涂抹"讲，不是"绘画"
• lay down = 铺下，放下（lay 是 lie 的过去式，也是 lay 的原形——注意区分！）
lay（放/铺）→ laid → laid；lie（躺）→ lay → lain
📌 词汇：
• ink /ɪŋk/ n. 墨水，墨汁
• lay down 铺下，放平（过去分词 laid）
7A printed sheet was created this way.
通过这种方式创建了一张印刷页。
🔤 句型：被动语态 + 方式状语（指代前文）
A printed sheet was created：被动语态，强调结果（印刷页）而非操作者
this way：方式状语，指代前面几句描述的整个印刷流程（排字→涂墨→铺纸）

📌 this way 的用法：前指功能，总结前文步骤，是说明文的常见收束方式。等同于 "in this way" 或 "by doing this"。
📌 词汇：
• printed sheet 印刷页，印张
• create /kriˈeɪt/ v. 创造，创建
8This technique improved the efficiency of printing and transformed traditional transcription into modern printing.
这项技术提高了印刷效率，将传统抄写转变为现代印刷。
🔤 句型：主语 + 并列谓语（V1 + O1 and V2 + O2）
主语 This technique 带两个并列谓语：
① improved the efficiency of printing
② transformed traditional transcription into modern printing

📌 transform A into B：固定搭配，把A转变为B。
例：The invention transformed the world.（这项发明改变了世界。）

📌 efficiency of printing：介词 of 表示所属关系，"印刷的效率"。
📌 词汇：
• efficiency /ɪˈfɪʃənsi/ n. 效率（efficient adj. 高效的）
• transform ... into ... 把……转变为……
• transcription /trænˈskrɪpʃən/ n. 抄写，誊写
9Scripts and paintings spread widely in the world with the help of yìn shuā.
文字和绘画在印刷术（yìn shuā）的帮助下在世界范围内广泛传播。
🔤 句型：主语 + 谓语 + 地点状语 + 方式状语
Scripts and paintings：并列主语（文字与绘画）
spread widely in the world：spread 是不规则动词（spread-spread-spread），此处用一般过去时
with the help of yìn shuā：介词短语作状语，表示"借助……的帮助"

📌 with the help of：固定搭配，= with the assistance of，表示凭借某种手段或力量。
📌 yìn shuā 是中文"印刷"的拼音，文章在此用拼音指代印刷技术。
📌 词汇：
• script /skrɪpt/ n. 文字，手稿，脚本
• spread /spred/ v. 传播，蔓延（三态同形：spread-spread-spread）
• with the help of 在……的帮助下

📝语法要点

1. 被动语态的使用

文章大量使用被动语态描述发明过程：

"Characters were carved on each piece of clay"
"They were fixed on an iron plate"
"Then ink was painted and a piece of paper was laid down"

语法规则：被动语态 = be + 过去分词

用法场景：强调动作对象而非执行者，常用于说明文、科技文献

2. 一般过去时描述历史事件

"Bi Sheng invented movable type print"
"This technique improved the efficiency"

语法规则：描述过去发生的、已完成的动作

3. 介词搭配

  • with frames - 用框架（工具）
  • in certain sequence - 按特定顺序
  • with the help of - 在...的帮助下

🎯关键理解点

1. 毕昇发明的核心理念

"movable" - 可移动性是最关键的创新点

  • 之前的印刷：整块木板雕刻 → 无法重复使用
  • 毕昇的发明：单个字模可自由组合 → 可重复使用

2. 技术细节流程

clay cubes → carve characters → arrange → fix on plate → ink → paper → print
(粘土块) (刻字符) (排列) (固定铁板) (涂墨) (铺纸) (印刷)

3. 历史影响

  • 效率提升：从抄写到印刷
  • 传播加速：scripts and paintings spread widely
  • 文明推动：为知识传播和文明发展奠定基础

🌏文化背景

毕昇 (Bi Sheng) - 北宋时期发明家

  • 时间：1041-1048年（北宋庆历年间）
  • 发明：活字印刷术 - 中国四大发明之一
  • 材料演进：粘土 → 木材 → 铜活字 → 铅活字
  • 传播路径：中国 → 朝鲜 → 欧洲（古腾堡，1450年左右）

✍️仿写练习

题目：描述一项发明的过程

模板：

[Inventor] invented [invention] around [time].
The [components] are made of [material].
[Parts] were [action] on each [component].
They could be [action] to [purpose].
They were fixed on [tool] in certain sequence.
Then [action] was taken and [result] was created.
This technique improved [aspect] and transformed [field].

示例（描述造纸术）：

Cai Lun invented paper around 2,000 years ago.
The raw materials are tree bark, hemp, and rags.
Fibers were extracted from each material.
They could be mixed to form different textures.
They were spread on a flat surface in certain thickness.
Then pressure was applied and the paper was dried.
This technique improved the efficiency of writing and transformed knowledge preservation.

📌阅读技巧

1. 识别说明文结构

  • 是什么 (invention, inventor, time)
  • 怎么做 (materials, process, steps)
  • 为什么重要 (impact, significance)

2. 关注连接词

  • then - 表示时间顺序
  • with the help of - 表示手段/工具
  • this way - 指代前文描述的方法

3. 提取关键信息（5W1H法）

  • Who: Bi Sheng
  • When: around 1,000 years ago
  • What: movable type print
  • How: clay cubes → arrange → ink → paper
  • Why: improve efficiency, spread knowledge

🔍难点解析

1. "which looked like the seals widely used in China"

结构分析：

  • main clause: "Characters were carved on each piece of clay"
  • non-restrictive relative clause: "which looked like the seals"
  • past participle phrase: "widely used in China" (修饰seals)

翻译：每个粘土块上刻有字符，看起来像中国广泛使用的印章

2. "with the help of yìn shuā"

yìn shuā = 印刷 (拼音)

句意：在印刷术的帮助下
