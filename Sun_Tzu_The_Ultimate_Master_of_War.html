📄 全文预览

Sun Tzu, who lived in China during the Spring and Autumn Period, is regarded as the ultimate master of war. His great military work, The Art of War, has been read for more than 2,000 years.

Sun Tzu believed that competition between economies and logistics is the nature of war. Only if the nation is prosperous and its people are strong enough will they win the war, otherwise the war will bring huge damages to the nation and people.

Gathering information on your enemies is the key to winning wars. The victorious general must be familiar with his enemies just as with himself and direct troops according to the newest situation.

The supreme art of war is to subdue the enemy without force. His spirit reminds us that peace is the common objective we should pursue.

🔍 语法拆解

  • 句型：复合句（主句 + 非限制性定语从句）
  • 主句主语：Sun Tzu
  • 主句谓语：is regarded as（一般现在时被动语态）
  • 主句表语：the ultimate master of war
  • 定语从句：who lived in China during the Spring and Autumn Period（修饰 Sun Tzu，提供背景信息）
  • 标点符号：定语从句前后有逗号，是非限制性定语从句，可以省略
💡 语法要点：非限制性定语从句
用逗号隔开，对先行词作补充说明（去掉后主句意思仍完整）。
例句：My brother, who lives in Beijing, is a teacher.
对比限制性定语从句（无逗号）：The man who lives in Beijing is my brother.（去掉后不知道是哪个man）

🔍 语法拆解

  • 句型：复合句（主句 + 宾语从句）

  • 宾语从句：that competition between economies and logistics is the nature of war（that 可省略）

  • 从句谓语：is（一般现在时，表达普遍真理，不受主句过去时影响）
⚠️ 表达问题：competition between economies and logistics
这句话的逻辑不太好理解——"经济体之间的竞争"和"后勤"之间是什么关系？
孙子原意应是"战争的本质是经济与后勤的较量"，但英文表达成了"economies 和 logistics 之间的 competition"，意思变成了"各经济体之间的竞争和后勤是战争的本质"，逻辑有歧义。
更好的表达：Sun Tzu believed that war is essentially a contest of economies and logistics.

  • CET-6 logistics (n.)：后勤，物流。军事语境中指"军需补给"。源自希腊语"logistikos"（计算、推理）
  • CET-4 nature (n.)：本质，性质。搭配：the nature of... …的本质
  • CET-4 competition (n.)：竞争。动词：compete；形容词：competitive

🔍 语法拆解

  • 句型：并列复合句（Only if 条件句 + otherwise 并列句）
  • 条件状语从句：Only if the nation is prosperous and its people are strong enough（放在句首，主句需倒装）
  • 主句（倒装）：will they win the war（正常语序：they will win → 倒装：will they win）
  • 并列句2：otherwise the war will bring huge damages to the nation and people（otherwise 引导相反情况的结果）
  • 时态：条件句用一般现在时（is, are），主句用将来时（will win, will bring）
💡 语法要点：Only if 开头的倒装句
当 "only + 状语" 放在句首时，主句需要倒装（助动词/情态动词提前）。
正常语序：They will win the war only if the nation is prosperous.
倒装语序：Only if the nation is prosperous will they win the war. ✅
类似用法：Only then did I understand.（直到那时我才明白。）
⚠️ 用词问题：huge damages
"damage" 作为"损害"讲时，通常是不可数名词，不用复数 damages。
damages（复数形式）是法律术语，意思是"赔偿金"（如：pay damages for the accident）。
正确表达：bring huge damage（不可数）或 cause great harm。
例句：The war brought huge damage to the country. ✅

⚠️ 文章主要问题汇总

问题1：标题表达不自然 — "Ultimate Master of War"
"master of war" 这个表达不太自然，通常会说 "master of warfare"（军事战略大师）或 "greatest military strategist"（最伟大的军事家）。
建议修改标题为：Sun Tzu: The Greatest Military Strategist 或 Sun Tzu and The Art of War。
问题2：逻辑不清 — "competition between economies and logistics"（句3）
这句话的意思模糊——"经济体之间的竞争"和"后勤"之间是什么关系？孙子原意应是"战争的本质是经济与后勤的较量"，但英文表达有歧义。
建议改为：Sun Tzu believed that war is essentially a contest of economies and logistics.
问题3：不可数名词误用复数 — "huge damages"（句4）
"damage" 作"损害"讲时是不可数名词，不能用复数 damages（法律术语"赔偿金"除外）。
正确：bring huge damage ✅   错误：bring huge damages ❌
建议改为：cause great harm 或 bring huge damage。
问题4：用词不当 — "newest situation"（句6）
"newest" 通常指"最崭新的/最新型的"，描述"最新情况"时更常用 latest。
建议改为：according to the latest situation ✅
问题5：句子过长，逻辑关系不清晰（句4）
句4把"只有国家繁荣人民强大才能打赢"和"否则战争会带来巨大损害"放在一个句子里，用了逗号连接两个独立分句，这是逗号拼接错误（comma splice）。
建议改为两句：Only if the nation is prosperous and its people are strong enough will they win the war. Otherwise, the war will bring huge damage to the nation and its people.
